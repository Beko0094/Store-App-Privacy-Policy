# Privacy Policy for المتجر الطبي (Medical Store App)

**Last Updated**: September 27, 2024

---

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to المتجر الطبي (Medical Store), a mobile application for purchasing pharmaceutical and medical products. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our App.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

**Account & Transaction Information**
- Name, email address, phone number
- Shipping and billing addresses
- Payment information (processed through secure third-party payment gateways)
- Order history and preferences
- Customer service inquiries and communications

**Image Information**
- Photographs uploaded through the App (e.g., prescription images, medical documentation)
- These images are processed and verified by our backend system

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

**Device Information**
- Device model and manufacturer
- Operating system version (Android SDK version)
- Device unique identifiers
- Mobile network information

**Usage & Activity Information**
- Pages viewed and products browsed
- Search queries
- Items added to shopping cart
- Time and duration of App usage
- App crashes and performance metrics

**Connectivity Information**
- Network connectivity status (WiFi, cellular, or no connection)
- IP address
- Internet service provider

**Location Information**
- We do not access precise GPS location
- We may use network-based location to serve localized content

### 2.3 Information from Device Permissions

When you grant permissions, we may access:
- **Camera**: To capture prescription photos or medical documents for order verification
- **Storage**: To access photos from your device gallery for upload
- **Internet Access**: To communicate with our servers

## 3. How We Use Your Information

### 3.1 Primary Uses
- Process orders and deliver products
- Process payments and prevent fraud
- Verify prescriptions and medical documentation
- Provide customer support
- Send order confirmations and shipping updates
- Improve App performance and user experience

### 3.2 Additional Uses
- Personalize your shopping experience
- Send marketing communications (with your consent)
- Conduct analytics and trend analysis
- Comply with legal and regulatory requirements
- Enforce terms of service
- Resolve disputes and troubleshoot technical issues

### 3.3 Image Processing
- Prescription and medical images are:
  - Verified for legitimacy and safety compliance
  - Temporarily stored for verification purposes
  - Used to ensure compliance with medical regulations
  - Securely deleted after verification completion

## 5. Data Security

### 5.1 Security Measures
- HTTPS encryption for all App-to-server communications
- Secure authentication protocols
- Regular security audits and updates
- Restricted access to sensitive information
- Secure file uploads for image verification

### 5.2 Limitations
While we implement industry-standard security measures, no system is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

### 5.3 Payment Security
